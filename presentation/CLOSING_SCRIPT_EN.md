## Recap: preserve meaning and provenance

Ontology makes the intended vocabulary explicit, while qualified statements keep each claim’s conditions and sources inspectable. Model and prompt choices changed the extracted views. Across 317 questions, downstream score changes were mixed: higher on 66, equal on 193 and lower on 58. Preserve competing contexts, and evaluate the answer before calling a larger graph an improvement.

The second lesson concerns the cost of delivering that evidence.

## Recap: engineer evidence delivery

Independent reads can overlap while delivering the same evidence, even when total storage work stays similar. Rust lowered the median in our fixture, while its tail was higher at concurrency four. These are database and client timings, with model generation excluded. Measure payload parity, the execution plan, median and tail together; neither language nor federation guarantees a universal speedup.
